Overview

This app is used to inspect Jabra Speak huddle/meeting room devices every onsite shift across IBM office buildings. Each room is marked Good or Issue and submitted to the cloud database.

UPA Building GY Shift Graveyard shift inspectors

Auto-save: Draft is auto-saved — refreshing the page will not lose your in-progress checklist.
Step-by-Step Inspection Workflow
  1. Open the app — tap the home screen icon if installed as a PWA, or navigate to the app URL in your browser.
  2. Go to the CHECKLIST tab and select your building/site from the dropdown: 1800 / UPA / NAGA.
  3. Click "DATE NOW" to auto-populate today's date and current time.
  4. Select your name from the Inspector dropdown.
    ⚠ Inspector name is required before submitting.
  5. For each room: tap ✓ Good or ✗ Issue.
    All rooms must be checked before submitting.
  6. For Issue rooms: select a preset remark or type a custom note in the text field.
  7. Click "SUBMIT DETAILS" when all rooms are checked.
  8. Cloud sync happens automatically after submission. A reminder notification fires after the configured delay (default: 2 min) for any Remarked rooms.
Status Reference
✓ Good
Device in good condition, proper position, locked — no action needed.
  • Jabra is in its designated spot
  • Device is locked/secured
  • No visible damage or battery issue
✗ Issue
Device requires attention — add a remark note.
  • Device displaced from normal position
  • Room occupied with no line of sight to Jabra
  • Battery low warning
  • Lock detached or missing
Preset Remarks (Issue rooms)
Occupied but Jabra is visible
Occupied and no vision to Jabra
Locked but Jabra is visible
Other issue (specify in text field)

Install as PWA
iOS Safari Only
  1. Open the app URL in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (box with ↑ arrow)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add to confirm
Android Chrome
  1. Open the app URL in Chrome
  2. Tap the install prompt (if shown) or Menu → "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap Install / Add to confirm
Once installed, the app icon appears on your home screen for quick launch — no browser needed.
Important Notes & Reminders
Before Submitting
  • All rooms must be marked Good or Issue
  • Inspector name must be selected
  • Issue rooms must have a remark note
Auto-Save & Sync
  • Draft is auto-saved — page refresh is safe
  • Cloud sync runs automatically after submit
  • Settings sync across all devices automatically
Alerts & Notifications
  • Email alert fires after configured delay (default 2 min)
  • Only Remarked (Issue) rooms trigger alerts
  • Use Settings → Test Email Alert to verify
